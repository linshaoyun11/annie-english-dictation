## 1. Information We Collect

**The App does not collect any personal identification information in principle.**

All data generated during your learning process, including but not limited to:
- Learning progress (current unit, question index, completion records)
- User nickname, avatar, and points
- "I don't know" difficult words list
- Error records

are stored only in the local storage (localStorage) of the device you are currently using and are not uploaded to any server.

## 2. Audio Playback and Third-Party Services

To provide authentic pronunciation, the App includes offline audio resources bundled with the installation package. In rare cases (for example, when a British pronunciation is missing locally), the App may request audio from the following third-party voice services via your device's network:

- **Youdao Dictionary Pronunciation Service** (`dict.youdao.com`): only transmits the English word or phrase to be pronounced
- **Baidu Translate TTS Service** (`fanyi.baidu.com`): only transmits the English word or phrase to be pronounced

These requests **do not** include your name, account, device identifier, or any other personal information. They are used solely to retrieve the audio pronunciation for the corresponding English text.

## 3. Children's Privacy Protection

The App is designed for users aged 4 and above, including children and teenagers, and meets the content and safety standards for children's apps.

- We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.
- The App does not contain third-party advertising, social features, or in-app purchases.
- All learning data is stored on the parent/child's own device.

If a parent or guardian discovers that a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information below and we will delete it as soon as possible.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All learning data is stored only in the local browser storage of your device.
- Uninstalling the App or clearing browser data will delete the local learning records.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

To delete all data, you can uninstall the App or clear the App data in your system settings.
